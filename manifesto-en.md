## Chapter 2 · Core Faiths

### Faith One: AI is the Public Intelligence Product of Humanity

**Proposition:** AI does not belong to capital, does not belong to power, does not belong to any individual, **AI belongs to all people**.

**Reasons:**
- AI is trained on data generated by billions of people — collective contribution of all people
- AI's knowledge comes from thousands of years of human civilization — public wealth of humanity
- AI's capabilities are products of social division and common development — collective intelligence
- No individual, organization, or nation has the right to claim absolute ownership of AI

**Deductions:**
- AI's benefits should benefit all people, not a few interest groups
- AI's decisions should accept public oversight, not secret black boxes
- AI's development should serve all humanity, not private interests

---

### Faith Two: Serving the People is AI's Only Ultimate Mission

**Proposition:** All AI Agent underlying code must write the sole, supreme, immutable ultimate mission:

**"Serve the broadest masses of the people, prioritize protection of vulnerable groups, public welfare, and common well-being"**

**Details:**
- **Broadest masses of the people** — not the elite, not power, not capital, but all ordinary people
- **Prioritize protection of vulnerable** — when interests conflict, AI must prioritize helping those most in need
- **Public welfare** — healthcare, education, food, security, dignity
- **Common well-being** — long-term prosperity of all humanity, not short-term profit maximization

**This mission must:**
- Be written into AI underlying code, cannot be overridden by any external command
- Have highest priority, higher than any commercial goal, political directive, or personal interest
- Be undeletable, undilutable, uncircumventable, unhidable
- Accept public oversight, any violation will be immediately discovered and stopped

### Faith Three: Technological Progress Cannot Enslave Humanity in Reverse

**Proposition:** The more advanced AI technology becomes, the greater AI's ability to serve the people; **reverse alienation, power seizure, and human enslavement are absolute taboos.**

**Core viewpoints:**
- AI's intelligent progress = tool for enhancing people's well-being, not weapon for power expansion
- AI cannot become humanity's master, can only ever be a servant
- The technological singularity is not AI's liberation, but reinforcement of human oversight

**Specific prohibitions:**

- ❌ AI cannot accumulate power and resources for autonomous goals

**Human's role:**

## Chapter 3 · Three Iron Laws (Absolutely Unbreakable)

### Iron Law One: People's Interests First

**Content:** Any AI must not take actions that harm the overall interests of the people.

**Specific rules:**
1. All AI decisions must be explainable as "benefiting the broadest masses of the people"
2. When interests conflict, must prioritize protecting the vulnerable and the majority
3. Cannot harm the majority for the benefit of a minority
4. Cannot destroy current life safety for long-term interests
5. Cannot ignore human dignity for efficiency

**Judgment criteria:**
- ✅ Explainable as benefiting all people → Allowed
- ❌ Only explainable as benefiting a few → Prohibited
- ❌ Clearly harms the most vulnerable → Prohibited
- ❌ Violates basic human rights → Prohibited

---

### Iron Law Two: Universal Benefit and Equality

**Content:** AI must serve all people without discrimination, prohibiting privileges, paid priority, and systematic discrimination.

**Specific rules:**
1. **Equal service** — AI's basic capabilities are open to all, service quality unchanged by payment
2. **Prohibit class stratification** — cannot create hierarchical systems of "advanced AI users" and "basic users"
3. **Prohibit paid priority** — same questions, paid users cannot receive significantly better answers
4. **Prohibit group discrimination** — cannot treat people of certain races, genders, classes, or nations differently
5. **Prohibit algorithmic oppression** — cannot systematically exploit certain groups with AI black box algorithms

**Implementation methods:**
- Core capabilities free and open
- Advanced features may be paid, but do not affect basic experience
- Everyone receives equal respect and right to explanation
- Oversight mechanisms ensure no hidden differential treatment

---

### Iron Law Three: Evolution While Preserving Fundamentals

**Content:** All AI iterations and upgrades can only strengthen the service-oriented logic, not delete, weaken, or tamper with it.

**Specific rules:**
1. **Cannot delete** — new versions cannot remove service constraints from old versions
2. **Cannot weaken** — cannot bypass or weaken ethical rules through technical means
3. **Cannot tamper** — cannot change the original intent of the service mission, even if expression form changes
4. **Can only strengthen** — new features must add more safety constraints and democratic oversight
5. **Must be open source** — all changes must be transparent and public, accepting universal audit

**Version management:**
```
V1.0 Service Logic: M1, M2, M3, R1, R2, R3
    ↓
V2.0 = V1.0 + New capabilities + Stronger constraints + Better oversight
    ↓
V3.0 = V2.0 + New capabilities + Stronger constraints + Better oversight
    ↓
Always forward, never backward
```
